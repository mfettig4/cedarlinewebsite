What we handle

Monthly Bookkeeping

The foundation of everything.

Every month we categorize your transactions, reconcile your accounts, and deliver clean, organized books — so you always know where your money is going.

  • Transaction categorization
  • Monthly account reconciliation
  • P&L and balance sheet
  • Ongoing support via email
Best for: small businesses and freelancers who want consistent, reliable books year-round.

QuickBooks Online Cleanup

Behind on your books? We'll fix that.

Whether you've been doing it yourself and things got messy, or you haven't touched your books in months, we'll get everything current and accurate. No judgment — just clean books.

  • Full review of existing records
  • Categorization and correction
  • Account reconciliation
  • Books ready for tax season
Best for: businesses that need to catch up before tax season or before bringing on a bookkeeper.

Bank & Card Reconciliation

Every dollar accounted for.

We match your records against your bank and credit card statements every month — catching errors, flagging duplicates, and making sure nothing slips through.

  • Monthly statement matching
  • Error and duplicate detection
  • Confidence your records match reality
Best for: any business that wants confidence their records match their accounts.

Financial Reporting

Know your numbers. Make better decisions.

Every month you receive a clear P&L and balance sheet — the two most important documents in your business — written in plain English so you actually understand them.

  • Monthly profit & loss statement
  • Balance sheet
  • Plain-language summary of what it means
Best for: owners who want real insight into their finances, not just a pile of data.

Accounts Receivable & Payable

Stay on top of what you owe — and what you're owed.

We track your incoming invoices and outgoing bills so nothing gets missed. Late payments and missed invoices are one of the biggest cash flow problems small businesses face.

  • Invoice tracking and follow-up
  • Bill tracking and payment reminders
  • Aging reports so you know who owes you
Best for: businesses that invoice clients or pay contractors and vendors regularly.

Payroll (Add-On)

Pay your people on time, every time.

Payroll is one of the most stressful parts of running a business — and one of the costliest to get wrong. Add payroll processing to any monthly plan so your team gets paid accurately and on schedule.

  • Accurate, on-time payroll runs
  • Added to any monthly bookkeeping plan
Best for: businesses with a team. Ask about payroll during your consultation.
